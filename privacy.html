Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026

This policy explains what information Teqard Labs Private Limited ("Teqard Labs", "we", "us") collects and how we use it. It covers:

  • our website, teqardlabs.com, and
  • our social media publishing tool at postiz.teqardlabs.com, which our team uses to schedule and publish posts to the social media accounts of Teqard Labs and its brands.

Our products have their own privacy policies, for example Epitree. Those policies apply when you use those products.

Information we collect

Contact form. When you use the contact form on teqardlabs.com, we receive your name, email address and message.

Social media publishing tool. The tool is used only by Teqard Labs staff. It is not open to the public. When we connect one of our social media accounts (for example a YouTube channel, Facebook Page, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Reddit, TikTok or Pinterest account), the platform shares the following with us:

  • the account's or channel's ID, name and profile picture;
  • access and refresh tokens that let the tool act on that account;
  • for YouTube, the basic Google profile (name and email address) of the person who authorised the connection;
  • the posts, videos and images we publish through the tool, and performance statistics for our own accounts (such as views and follower counts).

We do not use the tool to collect information about other people's accounts.

How we use information

  • Contact form details: to reply to your enquiry.
  • Social account data: only to publish and schedule content to the accounts we connected, manage that content, and review how it performs.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with data brokers.

Google and YouTube data

The publishing tool uses YouTube API Services to upload and manage videos and read channel statistics for our own channels. By connecting a YouTube channel, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Teqard Labs' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can revoke the tool's access to a Google account at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

Who we share information with

  • The social platform you connect. Content we publish is sent to that platform.
  • Service providers. Our contact form is processed by Formspree, and our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The publishing tool runs on servers we operate.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Storage, security and retention

Access tokens and account details are stored in the publishing tool's database. Only authorised Teqard Labs staff can access it, and all connections use HTTPS. We keep a connected account's data while that account is connected to the tool.

Contact form messages are kept only as long as needed to handle your enquiry.

Deleting your data

You can disconnect a social account by revoking the tool's access in that platform's settings. You can also email info@teqardlabs.com and ask us to delete any data we hold about you or your account. We will confirm deletion within 30 days.

Cookies

teqardlabs.com does not set advertising or analytics cookies. The publishing tool uses a login cookie for our staff.

Children

Our website and tools are not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above.
